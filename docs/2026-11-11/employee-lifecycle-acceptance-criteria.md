# Employee Lifecycle Acceptance Criteria

> Created: 2026-11-11

Define acceptance criteria for employee creation, onboarding, profile updates, department transfer, deactivation, reactivation, and offboarding. Include role permissions, required fields, audit expectations, and behavior when related records exist. Treat uncertain business rules as questions for confirmation.

## Evidence and review rules

- Inspect the current repository implementation before describing a behavior as implemented.
- Keep assumptions and unresolved business decisions explicit.
- Record commands, measurements, screenshots, and test outcomes only after they have actually been produced.
- Prefer reproducible steps and concrete expected outcomes over unsupported claims.
